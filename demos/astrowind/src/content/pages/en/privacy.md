## The short version

This is a static website. It has no database, no accounts and no tracking. Unless
you write to us, we do not know you visited.

## What we collect

**Nothing automatically.** No analytics script ships with the template, no cookies
are set, and no third-party fonts are loaded from remote servers — the typefaces
are served from this domain.

**What you send us.** If you use the contact form or write by email, we keep your
message and address for as long as it takes to answer you and a reasonable period
afterwards, in case the conversation continues.

## Server logs

The host serving these files may keep standard access logs — IP address, time and
requested URL — for security and troubleshooting. That is the host's processing,
governed by their policy, not ours.

## What we never do

We do not sell, rent or share your data. We do not build profiles. We do not use
your messages to train anything.

## Your rights

You can ask what we hold about you, ask for a copy, or ask us to delete it. Write
to the address on the contact page and we will act within thirty days.

## Changes

If this policy changes materially, the date at the top changes with it.
