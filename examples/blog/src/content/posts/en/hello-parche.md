Parche turns Astro into a framework you build on. This post is a plain Markdown
file in `src/content/posts/en`, rendered through the pluggable blog app.

## Pages are data

Content lives as JSON or Markdown. The blog app injects the routes, builds the
RSS feed and resolves taxonomies — no wiring required.
